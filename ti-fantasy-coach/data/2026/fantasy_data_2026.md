# TI 2026 Fantasy Challenge — Verified Data Reference

## 1. Scoring Rules (official in-game tooltip)

- Kill: +107 | Deaths: start 1,950, −195 per death | Last hit/deny (CS): +3 each
- GPM: player GPM × 2 | Madstone: +13 per stone | Tower: +352
- Sentry ward: +117 | Camp stack: +234 | Rune: +141 | Watcher: +147 | Lotus: +176 | Smoke: +293
- Roshan: +1,172 | Teamfight participation: up to 2,124 | Stun: +10/sec
- Tormentor: +879 | Courier kill: +703 | First blood: +1,934

**Settlement mechanics (critical):** Per position, final score = the **single highest-scoring series** in the settlement period, using the best 2 maps of that series. NOT a sum across series. Two-player positions are averaged per match. More series = more samples for the max, sublinear benefit. Spiky stats (Roshan, first blood, tormentor) get partially re-rated UP by max-selection.

## 2. Emblem System Rules (official)

- Quality tiers: T1/T2/T3/T4/T5 = +10%/+30%/+60%/+100%/+150%. Higher tiers rarer on rerolls.
- Traits: Fractal (+60% if ALL emblem qualities on banner differ) | Benevolent (+20% to adjacent emblems) | Vampiric (+50% self, −10% each neighbor) | Unique (+30% if only Unique on banner) | Friendly (+50% each when ≥3 Friendly on banner)
- Trait rerolls always produce a different trait. Stat rerolls always produce a different stat; old stat returns to pool. Quality & trait persist through stat rerolls (slot assets).
- Button wording: no "one emblem" qualifier = affects ALL emblems of that color; "first/last/random one" = single emblem.
- "Upgrade two qualities, lower one" button: observed downgrade is −2 tiers minimum. T5 (max tier) assets can only appear on the downgrade die.
- Team switching keeps all emblems intact, costs nothing, doesn't refresh the option pool. Every craft refreshes the entire pool for free. Pool refresh button costs 1 token.

## 6. Community Meta Findings (guides + 100k-game statistical study)

- Blue stats are highly predictable; green stats are coin-flips; courier/teamfight/stuns/tormentor/first blood/Roshan are luck- or draft-dependent.
- Madstone (13/stone) only spikes in marathon games where cores farm reroll tokens; rare. Confirmed trap.
- Deaths score is stable but only suits teams with sacrificial mids (e.g., Iron Wing, occasionally Falcons).
- Guides rate Tormentor/Roshan up under best-series-selection because the best series usually contains multiple.
- External calculators: ti2026calculator.com (1,601 T1 matches, player-level projections) and battlepass.ru/en/ti2026/fantasy-calc (2,888 replays parsed; notes official API miscounts: watchers over-counted 1.5×, madstones under-counted 3×, lotus loses ~20%, tormentor credited to last-hitter though game credits all participants).
